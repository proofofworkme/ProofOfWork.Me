# Search product release — 2026-10-04 UTC

`search.proofofwork.me` and Computer's `/?folder=search`. It discovers current
and historical Computer protocol data, public content, verified readable files
and source transaction evidence without a wallet. Confirmed valid records are
the default; explicit status/validity filters expose pending, historical invalid
and raw-carrier evidence. Queries and source selection are shareable URL state.

Search is an additive disposable projection over existing validator events,
raw OP_RETURN carriers/scripts, source transactions and verified attachments.

canonical replay or confirmed history. Searches and indexed copies add no
payment or second Growth/WORK contribution. Raw decoding alone does not confer
semantic validity; historical protocol forms and invalid attempts remain
inspectable. Exact proof amounts remain decimal strings.
